# Kakuzi PLC: Equity Research, Financial Analysis & Climate Risk Assessment
**Project Status:** In Progress

**Research Period:** FY2021–FY2025

**Company:** Kakuzi PLC

**Market:** Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE)

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel | Power BI | Financial Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project involves conducting an independent equity research and financial analysis of **Kakuzi PLC,** a Kenyan agricultural company listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange.

The objective is to evaluate the company's financial performance, operational drivers, business segments, and exposure to climate-related risks. The analysis will combine historical financial statement analysis, segment performance assessment, financial forecasting, valuation scenarios, and sustainability considerations.

The project is designed to demonstrate practical skills in financial modelling, business research, data analysis, and communicating investment-related insights.

This is an ongoing research project. The repository will be updated as additional analysis and deliverables are completed.

## 2. Research Objectives
The project seeks to:

- Assess Kakuzi's historical financial performance and financial position.
- Analyse revenue composition and the performance of its key business segments.
- Evaluate profitability, liquidity, leverage, efficiency, and cash flow generation.
- Examine the company's operational and geographical revenue exposure.
- Assess relevant climate-related risks and their potential implications for business performance.
- Develop financial forecasts and valuation scenarios based on stated assumptions.
- Communicate findings through a research report, executive summary, and interactive dashboard.

## 3. Scope of Analysis
The research covers the following areas:

|AREA|FOCUS|
| --- | --- |
|Company Overview|Business model, operations, products and market exposure|
|Financial Statement Analysis| Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement|
|Financial Ratios| Profitability, liquidity, leverage, efficiency and growth|
|Segment Analysis| Revenue, profitability and asset allocation across business segments|
|Climate Risk Assessment| Climate exposure, operational implications and sustainability considerations|
|Financial Modelling| Historical analysis, assumptions and projected financial performance|
|Valuation| Valuation scenarios and sensitivity analysis|
|Data visualisation|Interactive Power BI dashboard and presentation of key findings|
